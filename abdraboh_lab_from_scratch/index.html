About the lab

Experimental biology with a translational vision

Abdraboh Lab investigates molecular and cellular mechanisms involved in cancer biology, cell signaling, stem cell biology, neurodegenerative disease, and environmental toxicology. The lab integrates experimental models with molecular approaches to understand disease mechanisms and evaluate potential therapeutic strategies.
