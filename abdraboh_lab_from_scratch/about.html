About Abdraboh Lab

A research laboratory focused on molecular biology, cancer biology, cell signaling, and translational biomedical research at Mansoura University.

Mission

Understanding disease mechanisms through molecular research

Abdraboh Lab aims to advance understanding of the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying cancer progression, neurodegenerative disease, stem cell responses, receptor signaling, and environmentally induced tissue injury.

The lab combines molecular biology, tissue culture, disease models, biochemical analysis, and histological approaches to generate research outputs with biomedical relevance.

Principal investigator

Prof. Mohamed Elsaid Abdraboh

Prof. Mohamed Elsaid Abdraboh is Head of Zoology Department, Faculty of Science, Mansoura University. His academic and research profile spans molecular biology, cancer biology, tissue culture, stem cell biology, and disease-related molecular signaling.

His research contributions include work on CD44-mediated breast cancer invasion and metastasis, melatonin-associated signaling, anticancer phytochemicals, stem cell modulation, and experimental disease models.
